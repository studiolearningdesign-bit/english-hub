The Story Behind Your Name

Real names, funny nicknames, and the first hello when you finally meet someone in person.

Teacher's Guide · 01 Warm-Up P Only
1. SetupGreet the student using Hello! and What's your name? Keep strictly to [P] frames.
2. ModelingClick one reaction option yourself first: "My name is [Name]. Yes, it is good!"
3. CheckHave the student click their answers and say one short [P] line aloud.
4. KeysOpen-ended personal activation. Accept any valid Yes / No / My name is... response.

Teacher's Guide · 03 USA Chunks Fixed Expressions
1. SetupTeach these 4 expressions as whole, fixed blocks. Do NOT analyze the grammar inside them.
2. ModelingPlay each audio and drill natural rhythm: "You can CALL me Lupe."
3. CheckSay a long or funny name and prompt the student to react with "Again, please?" or "No, I'm serious!"
4. KeysStudents should use at least two chunks naturally in Module 06 Speaking.

Teacher's Guide · 04 Listening Transcript Hidden First
1. SetupKeep the transcript hidden! Play the studio audio once for gist, then a second time for details.
2. ModelingAsk: "Is his name Mateo? Yes or No?" before checking all 4 items.
3. CheckHave the student answer Yes/No, then open the transcript to notice I'm / I'm not / Are you...?
4. Keys1. No (he is Mateo) · 2. Yes (real name: Mateo) · 3. No (nickname: Teo) · 4. Yes (last name is hard).
04 · Notice in Context

At the Studio Check-In

Listen to Nora and Mateo first. Do not open the text until you finish the questions.

Audio Track: Studio Check-In
Nora & Mateo meet for the first time.

Nora: Good morning! I'm Nora. What's your name?

Mateo: Hi, Nora! Nice to finally put a face to the name. I'm Mateo.

Nora: Mateo... Are you Mateo Valdovinos?

Mateo: Yes, I am! My last name is hard. You can call me Teo.

Nora: Okay, Teo! Wait—is your nickname Kiwi on the list?

Mateo: Haha, no! I'm not Kiwi. I'm Teo!

Nora: Oh, sorry! Again, please? Teo?

Mateo: Yes! Teo. No, I'm serious—Valdovinos is hard, Teo is easy!

Teacher's Guide · 05 Grammar I / you + To Be + My
1. SetupScope strictly limited to I, you + am / are + My. Do NOT introduce he/she/they here.
2. ModelingPoint out am goes with I, and are goes with you.
3. CheckIn Error Clinic, let the student find the mistake without hints. Then build sentences in the Wordbox.
4. KeysClinic: 1. I am / I'm Carlos · 2. You are / You're funny · 3. Are you Mateo? · 4. I'm not Kiwi · 5. My name is Nora.
05 · Understand & Practice

I, You & To Be

Use I am (I'm) to talk about yourself, and You are (You're) to talk to another person. Use My for your name.

Form I (+ My) You
Affirmative (+) I am Mateo. / I'm Teo.
My name is Mateo.		 You are Nora. / You're Nora.
Negative (−) I am not Kiwi. / I'm not Kiwi. You are not Teo. / You aren't Teo.
Yes/No Question (?) Am I on the list?
Yes, you are. / No, you aren't.		 Are you Mateo?
Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.

Teacher's Guide · 06 Speaking Roleplay + Checklist
1. SetupStudent picks one Secret Name Card below (do not show partner). You play the check-in host.
2. ModelingAsk: "Hello! Are you Leo?" Student answers with No, I'm not... and uses a chunk.
3. CheckVerify the 4 checklist items are demonstrated aloud without reading from a script.
4. KeysEvidence: 1 greeting, 1 negative correction (I'm not), 1 chunk, 1 question back (What's your name?).

Speaking Checklist

1. Say hello and ask your partner's name (What's your name?).
2. Say your real name with I'm... or My name is...
3. Correct one wrong guess with No, I'm not...
4. Use at least one USA Chunk (You can call me... / No, I'm serious! / Again, please?).
Teacher's Guide · 07 Exit Ticket Evidence Check
1. SetupStudent writes 3–4 isolated sentences in the blank space without copying from earlier tabs.
2. ModelingPrompt orally: "Write your name, your nickname (real or fun), and one 'I'm not' sentence."
3. CheckCheck capital I, capital names, apostrophe in I'm / I'm not, and period/question mark.
4. KeysAdvance only if student produces accurate I am / I'm not and My name is... independently.
07 · Reflect

Write 3 or 4 short sentences about your name, your nickname, and one thing you are not.
