Small-town pride, big-city energy, and the difference between your roots and your home today.

04 · Notice in Context

Three Profiles: Hometown vs. Home Today

Read the three profiles silently first. Then listen to check pronunciation and answer the questions.

Teacher Sequence · Activity 4A: Silent Reading & Optional Post-Read Audio Inductive Notice
Propósito pedagógico: Contrastar from (origen) y home is in (residencia) en perfiles reales e introducir inductivamente he's, she's, we're, they're antes del Módulo 05.
1. Lectura silenciosa primeroDa 60–90 segundos para leer en silencio. No reproduzcas el audio hasta después de leer.
2. Escucha opcional post-lecturaUsa los botones Listen (after reading) para modelar ritmo y acento en nombres de ciudades (Ce-LA-ya, MÉ-ri-da, Me-de-LLÍN).
3. Puente a GramáticaSeñala en los perfiles: "Diego → He's from Celaya. Camila → She's Colombian. Lucas & Elena → They're from Canada."
Diego
Celaya → Mexico City

"Hello! I'm Diego. Born and raised in Celaya! Celaya is small, warm, and friendly. Now, my home is in Mexico City. It's big and busy, not quiet!"

Camila
Medellín, Colombia

"Hi! I'm Camila. I'm Colombian—born and raised in Medellín! My neighborhood is warm and friendly. My friend in Toronto is from Medellín too—small world! What about you?"

Lucas & Elena

"Hi! We're Lucas and Elena. We're Canadian—born and raised in Toronto! Toronto is big and modern. Now, we're in Mérida. It's warm and quiet!"

Teacher Sequence · Activity 4B: Profile Comprehension Check Origin vs. Home · Keys
Propósito pedagógico: Verificar que el alumno no confunde origen (from) con residencia actual (home is in).
1. Si hay error en #1Regresa al perfil de Diego y contrasta las dos frases: "born and raised in Celaya" vs. "my home is in Mexico City".
2. Answer Keys1. No (Celaya) · 2. Yes · 3. No (Canadian / Toronto) · 4. Yes (Mérida).
05 · Understand & Practice

He, She, We, They + To Be & Yes/No Questions

Use is with he / she, and are with we / they / you. Put Are / Is first to ask a Yes/No question.

Teacher Sequence · Activity 5A: Guided Discovery (He/She/We/They + Yes/No) Strict Scope [N]
Límite estricto [F]: Prohibido Where do you come from?, Where do you live?, o posesivos his/her/their. Enseña Where are you from? como marco fijo de origen.
1. Anclaje con retratosSeñala cada tarjeta visual: Diego → He is (He's). Camila → She is (She's). You + I → We are (We're). Lucas & Elena → They are (They're).
2. Interferencia L1Anticipa dos errores: 1) usar is con plural ("They is from Canada"), 2) olvidar invertir en pregunta ("You are from León?" → "Are you from León?").
3. Respuestas cortasDestaca que en afirmativo corto no se contrae: "Yes, he is" (nunca "Yes, he's"). En negativo sí: "No, he isn't."

City Mixer: Who Am I? (Origin Guessing Game)

Teacher Sequence · Activity 6A: Information-Gap Guessing Game Performance Task
Propósito pedagógico: Evaluar producción oral real de Is he/she from...? / Are they from...? / Yes, he is / No, she isn't con vacío de información auténtico (distinto al Roleplay de L01).
1. Ronda 1 (Docente pregunta)El alumno elige una tarjeta en secreto. Tú preguntas: "Is she from Colombia?" / "Are they from Canada?" El alumno responde solo con Yes, she is / No, he isn't.
2. Ronda 2 (Alumno pregunta)Inviertan roles: tú eliges tarjeta en secreto y el alumno debe formular al menos 2 preguntas con Is he/she...? o Are they...?
3. Cierre personalCierra con 1 pregunta real: "Where are you really from? Is your home in the same city?"

Speaking Checklist

1. Ask at least 2 Yes/No questions with Is he/she from...? or Are they from...?
2. Answer accurately with Yes, he/she is or No, he/she isn't (or they are / aren't).
3. Say where you are from and where your home is today.
4. Use at least one Everyday Expression (Born and raised in... / Small world! / No way! / What about you?).
