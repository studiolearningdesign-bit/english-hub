05 · Language Lab

The Structure Behind the Question

Use am, is, and are to check names, locations, positions, and status.

Subject Affirmative Negative Yes/No Wh-
I I am ready. I am not ready. Am I ready? Where am I?
He · She · It She is ready. She isn't ready. Is she ready? Where is she?
You · We · They They are ready. They aren't ready. Are they ready? Where are they?
Interactive Builder

Change the Subject

1. Where are you? → New subject: Rachel
2. How old are you? → New subject: he
3. Is Rachel available? → New subject: they
4. Where is the manager? → New subject: the managers
Possessive Transfer

• What is your position? → What is her position?
• What are your strengths? → What are their strengths?
Say the questions aloud with his, her, and their.

01 · Interview Analysis

Candidate Profile

Candidate: Jordan Ellis

I'm a finance professional. My area of expertise is accounting. I'm organized and responsible. I'm a good candidate for this position because I'm reliable.

1. Who are you as a professional?
2. What is your area of expertise?
3. What are your strengths?
4. Why are you a good candidate for this position?

02 · Professional Profile Builder

Expertise = your professional specialty. Strengths = things you are good at. Candidate = a person in an interview for a job. Reliable = responsible and dependable.
