Before a client audit, laptops, keycards, and confidential folders are mixed up across the office. Identify who owns each item and who has it now.

Intelligibility Focus

Pronouncing 's (Ownership) & His / Her

Do not drop the 's sound at the end of a name! And remember: his = a man's item; her = a woman's item.

Mark's / Daniel's /s/ · /z/ "Mark's badge. Daniel's laptop."
Carlos's / Her /ɪz/ · /hɜːr/ "Carlos's charger. Her keycard."

03 · USA Corporate Chunks

3 Office Boundary & Ownership Blocks

Use these 3 fixed workplace expressions as complete blocks.

"Who left this here?" Inquiry
/huː left ðɪs hɪr/
There's a charger on the desk. Who left this here?
Flip ↻
Pragmatic Meaning
"Who left this here?"

Natural way to ask who forgot or placed an item on a shared table or desk.

Fixed chunk Flip ↺
"Hands off!" Boundary
/hændz ɔːf/
That's Victoria's folder—hands off!
Flip ↻
Pragmatic Meaning
"Hands off!"

Firm, clear boundary meaning "do not touch or take this" (often playful with coworkers, or serious for confidential files).

Fixed chunk Flip ↺

Pragmatic Meaning
"I'll take care of it."

Professional way to take responsibility and promise to solve or store an item properly.

04 · Listening

The Pre-Audit Desk Check

Rachel, Daniel, and Sophie check the conference room before the CFO arrives. Answer the 3 questions before opening the transcript.

0:00 The Pre-Audit Desk Check 0:00

Comprehension Check

1. Whose laptop is in the conference room?
2. Whose charger is on the table?
3. Who has Victoria's confidential contract?
Audio Transcript

Sophie: "Wait—whose laptop is this? Who left this here?"

Rachel: "It's Daniel's laptop. He's in Operations, and his badge is next to it."

Sophie: "Okay. And whose charger is on the table? Is it his charger too?"

Rachel: "No, that's my charger! Thank you. I'll take care of it."

Daniel: "Hi, team. Do you have my keycard? I don't have it on my desk."

Sophie: "Yes, reception has your keycard. Wait—what's in this blue folder?"

Rachel: "Hands off! That's Victoria's confidential contract. I have her folder for the audit, and our finance team has the backup files."
