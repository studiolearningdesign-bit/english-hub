05 · Grammar in Context

Past Simple vs. Present Perfect (The Time-Impact Formula)

Use Past Simple to anchor when you delivered a project, and switch to Present Perfect to prove that your work is still creating value today.

Role in Your Pitch Affirmative Negative Questions
Past Simple
Specific finished milestone (In Q3, Last May)		 In May, I redesigned the client handoff. We didn't miss a single deadline in Q3. Did the new process launch on time?
When did you take over that account?
Present Perfect
Ongoing impact (Since then, Over the past 6 months)		 Since then, it has saved six hours a week. Client complaints haven't returned since June. How much time has this saved?
Have you taken on extra scope?
The Diplomatic Ask
Connect ongoing value to alignment		 Given that I've taken on extra scope, I'd like to align my role. I'm not asking for a change without clear results. Would next quarter work to review this?

06 · Live Communication

Live 1-on-1 Compensation Pitch

Use your own real achievements—or click one role card below—and deliver a calm 90-second pitch to your coach, then handle two realistic pushbacks.

OPTION A · YOUR REAL ROLE
Your Actual Achievements
• 1 project you completed in the last 6–12 months
• 1 ongoing result (time saved, quality, or growth)
• 1 clear alignment ask
OPTION B · OPERATIONS LEAD
Supply & Process Lead
• Q2: Automated daily warehouse checklists
• Since Q2: Cut dispatch delays by 25%, saved 8 hrs/week
• Extra scope: Training 3 regional coordinators
OPTION C · CLIENT SUCCESS
Senior Account Lead
• Last August: Built a 30-day client onboarding playbook
• Since then: Kept 95% renewal rate, absorbed 2 enterprise accounts
• Ask: Senior title & compensation band alignment

Be Ready for Two Realistic Boss Pushbacks:

1. "These are solid results, though isn't improving processes already part of your core role?"
2. "Budgets are tight this quarter—what if we revisit this in six months?"

Speaking Checklist (What Great Looks Like)

✓ Time-Impact contrast: Anchor your milestone in Past Simple and prove ongoing results with Present Perfect.
✓ Quantified value: Include at least one concrete number, percentage, or hours saved.
✓ Executive chunks: Use at least two expressions (on top of my core role, since we rolled that out, bottom-line impact, align on).
✓ Composed pushback: Respond calmly to the two objections without sounding defensive.
